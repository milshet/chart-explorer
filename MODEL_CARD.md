## Intended use

- **For:** answering a question about one finance or economics chart image (bar, line or pie charts of the kind Statista, Pew, OECD and Our World in Data publish), with the evidence it read and its reasoning shown, for a person who checks the answer against the chart.
- **Not for:** financial, investment or policy decisions without a human checking the chart; charts outside that style (no out-of-distribution set was built, see Limitations); scanned or photographed charts; documents with several charts; any language but English; questions whose answer is in text printed on the chart rather than in the plotted data (see Failure modes: captions).
- **Trust the abstention less than the answer.** The model detects missing information more often than it acts on it (Refusal behaviour).

## Failure modes

## Deployment

- **Latency** (L40S, one request at a time, 50 val requests over HTTP): p50 1.224 s, p95 1.847 s; HTTP adds 3 ms. No continuous batching.
- **Contract enforcement:** answers are validated against the contract and re-asked with the errors quoted. Over these requests 49/50 were valid first time, 1 retried, 1 fell back to a flagged abstention (`degraded` in the response envelope, never inside the answer).
- **Refused inputs:** caller-supplied context, tables or prompts (the endpoint takes a question and an image only), and images over 8 MB or 40 megapixels.
- **Container:** NOT MEASURED.

## Limitations

- **No out-of-distribution test.** No chart generator was built for an unseen-style evaluation set (Eval-B), so nothing here shows the model generalises beyond ChartQA's chart styles.
- **Small test set.** One accuracy on 180 items carries roughly ±7 points; claims rest on paired tests, and differences of a few points are not established.
- **English, finance and economics only,** from four publishers' chart styles.
